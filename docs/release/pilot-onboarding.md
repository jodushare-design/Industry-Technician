# Pilot Onboarding

How to bring the first rice mill and its technicians onto the platform. Start only when the "Before the pilot" items in [known-limitations.md](known-limitations.md) are closed.

**Pilot shape.** One rice mill (the customer) and three to five technicians or service companies who already work on rice mills in the area. Optionally, one general-industry factory later. The pilot runs on the **website in the phone browser**. The Android app is not ready.

## What to tell every participant

Use plain words, in Telugu or Hindi where that helps. The interface is English only for now, so a team member should sit with each person for their first sign-in.

- The platform helps customers **find and contact** independent technicians and service companies, chat with them, and keep a record of estimates, approvals, parts and completion.
- **The platform does not employ the technicians, send them, do the repair, sell parts or take payment.** The customer chooses the provider and pays the provider directly, as agreed between them. The provider is responsible for their own work.
- Badges such as "identity checked" mean only that the platform team checked that document. They are not a promise about the quality of work.
- The terms are **drafts awaiting legal review**. Show the draft banner and explain it, if the founder has decided to run the pilot on the drafts ([known-limitations.md](known-limitations.md)).
- Complaints: use **Make a complaint** on the job, or contact the Grievance Officer shown on the website. Acknowledgement is within 24 hours. Safety incidents: stop work, make the site safe, call emergency services if needed, then report it on the platform.

## Before the visit (platform team)

1. Approve the starter catalogue entries for the pilot mill's machines in **Admin → Catalogue**, after the technician review:
   - paddy cleaner, destoner, rubber roll husker, paddy separator, rice whitener;
   - rice polisher, rotary sifter, colour sorter, air compressor, electrical panel.
2. Prepare a one-page printout per participant: the website address, "sign in with your mobile number", and the support contact.
3. Agree the pilot period (for example 6 weeks), a weekly check-in time and one named support person.

## Onboarding the rice mill (customer)

With the mill owner or manager, on their phone:

1. Open the website → **Sign in** → enter their mobile number → enter the 6-digit SMS code.
2. Enter their name → choose **I need a technician** → read and accept the terms.
3. **My account → Factories → Add factory:** name, address, locality, district and PIN. Optionally use the phone's location for better matching.
4. **Add machines** at the factory, one by one: choose the machine type, give it the name the mill uses ("Husker 1"), and add the make and model if known. The ten pilot machines are listed above. *"I don't know the machine name"* is fine. Ten machines take about 10 minutes.
5. Walk through a **practice request**, then cancel it with the reason "Practice":
   - **Ask for help** → choose the machine → tick what is happening → describe it → add a photo.
   - **Find providers** → see who matches and why.
6. Show where to find: **My requests**, **Notifications**, an estimate's **Approve / Ask for changes / Reject** buttons, spare-part approval, **Confirm the job is done**, and **Make a complaint**.

## Onboarding technicians and service companies

With each technician, on their phone:

1. Sign in with their mobile number → name → choose **I am an independent technician**, or **I run a service company** → accept the terms.
2. **Profile:** display name, short description, years of experience, languages, availability.
3. **Service area:** base locality and district, travel radius, extra districts.
4. **Skills:** one row per machine they really work on, with their own years and level. Add make and model experience where they have it. Be specific: matching uses these facts and shows them to customers as "stated by provider".
5. **Documents:** upload an identity document. Optionally add certificates, licences (needed for electrical panels and other regulated work) and insurance. Documents are private: only the technician and platform staff can open them.
6. **Submit for review.**

**Service companies** also:
- fill in the business details (phone, address, GSTIN if registered);
- under **Team**, invite each employee by mobile number. The employee signs in with that number, accepts the invitation, completes their own profile and submits it.

**Verification (platform staff, in the admin portal):**
1. Open the **Verification queue** and review each document against the original, in person or by video call.
2. Record the identity check, then approve, or reject with a reason.
3. Only approved profiles appear in matching.
4. Aim to review within 2 working days. Reports shows the median review time.

Show each technician:
- **Requests for you:** accept or decline;
- chat;
- the estimate form: inspection fee only for an inspection; visit charge, labour, travel and tax for a repair; validity date;
- adding a spare part with its price and submitting it for the customer's approval;
- scheduling, the site-safety checklist, progress updates, before and after photos, and the completion report.

## During the pilot

- **Weekly check-in** with the mill and each provider. Note confusing screens, slow pages and anything that felt like the platform was taking a role it should not.
- **Support staff** watch the dashboard daily: verification queue, open complaints (acknowledge within 24 hours), disputed jobs.
- **Reports, every week:** requests, provider acceptance time, estimates approved, completions and complaints. Share the counts, not personal data, in the pilot review.
- Record bugs with the phone model, browser, page and time. Fix critical ones before inviting more users.

## Ending or extending the pilot

At the end, review with the founder:
- the test plan exit criteria ([pilot-test-plan.md §4](../pilot-test-plan.md#4-exit-criteria-for-inviting-the-pilot-customer));
- the open items in [known-limitations.md](known-limitations.md);
- the participants' feedback.

Data created during the pilot is kept under the privacy policy. Do not delete approvals, estimates, complaints or audit records.
