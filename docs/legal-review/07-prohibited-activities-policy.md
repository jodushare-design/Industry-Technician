## Prohibited activities (draft list)

### A. Misrepresentation
1. Claiming to be employed by, certified by, or endorsed by the platform.
2. Claiming qualifications, licences, certificates, insurance, brand authorisation or experience you do not have.
3. Uploading forged, altered, expired or someone else's documents.
4. Claiming a spare part is compatible, genuine/OEM or new without a basis.
5. Fake requests, fake estimates, fake reviews or fake completion reports.

### B. Unsafe or unlawful work
6. Offering or accepting work that requires a licence, approval or registration you do not hold. Examples: electrical installation work, boiler repairs, pressure vessels, lifts/hoists.
7. Arranging work designed to bypass statutory permits, inspections or safety measures.
8. Dealing in stolen, counterfeit, smuggled or restricted goods or parts.

### C. Abuse of the approval process
9. Starting or charging for work beyond the approved estimate without a new in-app approval.
10. Adding unapproved part costs to a bill.
11. Pressuring, threatening or misleading a customer into approving.

### D. Abuse of people and data
12. Harassment, threats, discrimination, abusive language, sexual content.
13. Collecting, selling or misusing other users' personal or business data. Spam. Unsolicited marketing.
14. Contacting users outside a job for unrelated purposes using details obtained through the platform.

### E. Platform abuse
15. Bribery or kickbacks in connection with platform jobs.
16. Scraping, bots, credential sharing, security attacks, circumventing suspension.
17. Malware or harmful files in uploads.

## Consequences (draft)

| Severity | Examples | Typical action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Forged documents, unsafe unlicensed regulated work, threats, fraud | Immediate suspension, evidence preserved, report to authorities where required |
| Serious | Charging unapproved work, false compatibility claims, data misuse | Suspension pending review |
| Minor | Off-topic content, minor profile inaccuracy | Warning, content removal, correction request |
