## Template outline

1. **Who we are.** [Legal entity name, CIN/LLPIN, registered office, Hyderabad, Telangana]. Contact details. Grievance Officer (see Grievance Policy).

2. **What the platform is.** A technology platform that helps you:
   - find independent technicians and service companies ("providers");
   - communicate with them;
   - receive and approve estimates;
   - coordinate spare parts;
   - raise complaints.

   See the Marketplace Service Description.

3. **What the platform is not.** We do not employ, dispatch or supervise providers. We do not diagnose faults, perform or supervise repairs, sell machinery or spare parts, or collect repair payments [in the current version of the service].

4. **Eligibility and accounts.**

   - Mobile OTP verification.
   - You are responsible for activity on your account and for your authorised users.

5. **Choosing a provider.**
   - You choose which providers to contact and engage.
   - Search results and "why this match" explanations are based on information providers give and on the checks shown on their profile. They are not a recommendation or guarantee.
   - Each verification badge means only what its label says, as of the date shown.

6. **Your agreement with the provider.**
   - Scope, price, schedule, warranty (if any) and payment are agreed directly between you and the provider.
   - The provider is responsible for technical assessment, diagnosis, method and workmanship.

7. **Your responsibilities.**
   - Give accurate machine information.
   - Have authority over the machine and site.
   - Arrange safe site access, isolation/lock-out, permits and site safety rules.
   - Tell the provider about known hazards.
   - Comply with laws that apply to you as the site occupier/employer.

8. **Estimates and approvals.**
   - Governed by the Service Estimate and Approval Terms.
   - Only your in-app approval is recorded as an approval.
   - Additional work and parts need your separate approval.

9. **Spare parts.** Governed by the Spare-Parts Coordination Terms. Compatibility must be confirmed by the responsible parties.

10. **Messaging and content.**
    - In-app chat is the official channel.
    - You give us a licence to host and process your uploads to run the service.
    - Don't upload third-party personal data or identity documents unnecessarily.

14. **Suspension and termination.** We may suspend or close accounts for misconduct, policy violations, fraud or legal requirements, [with notice and reasons where practicable]. Records needed for complaints, disputes or legal obligations are kept as described in the Privacy Policy.
