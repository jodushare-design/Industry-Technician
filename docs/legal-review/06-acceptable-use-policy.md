## Template outline

1. **Purpose.** To keep the platform useful, safe and lawful for industrial customers and providers.

2. **Use the platform for genuine industrial service needs.**
   - Post real requests.
   - Respond honestly.
   - Keep job discussions in the job chat.

3. **Be accurate.**
   - Machine details, problem descriptions, profiles, experience, documents, estimates and part compatibility must be truthful.
   - Mark anything you don't know as "unknown".

4. **Respect people.**
   - Professional, non-abusive communication.
   - No harassment, discrimination or threats.

5. **Respect privacy.**
   - Use other users' contact details and job information only for the job.
   - Don't record, publish or share other users' data without permission.

6. **Upload responsibly.**
   - Only content you have the right to share.
   - Only content relevant to the job.
   - Avoid capturing people or confidential production information unnecessarily.
   - Allowed file types and sizes as shown in the app.

7. **Use records properly.**
   - Approvals and estimates must be made in the app.
   - Don't pressure a customer to approve.
   - Don't treat a message as an approval.

8. **Safety.**
   - Never use the platform to arrange work that bypasses legally required permits, licences or safety measures.
   - Report incidents through the complaint channel (safety incident).

9. **Platform integrity.**
   - No scraping, automated access, reverse engineering, security testing without written permission, or overloading.
   - No multiple accounts to evade suspension.

10. **Enforcement.** We may take any of these actions, proportionate to the breach and [with notice and an opportunity to respond where practicable]:
    - remove content;
    - restrict features;
    - suspend or close accounts;
    - report to authorities.
