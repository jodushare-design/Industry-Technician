## Draft statement

1. **Independent business.** You use [Platform name] as an independent service provider. Nothing in your use of the platform makes you an employee, worker, agent, partner, joint venturer or franchisee of [Platform name].

2. **Your control over your work.** You alone decide:
   - whether to respond to or accept any request;
   - your prices, terms, schedule and working hours;
   - how to assess, diagnose and repair equipment;
   - which tools, equipment, parts and helpers to use;
   - whether to work for other clients or through other channels.

3. **What [Platform name] does not do.** It does not:
   - dispatch you to jobs;
   - set your prices;
   - supervise or inspect your work;
   - give you technical instructions;
   - provide your tools or uniforms;
   - require you to use its branding.

4. **Your legal responsibilities.** You are responsible for:
   - your qualifications, licences, registrations and insurance where required;
   - safe work practices;
   - your own taxes;
   - for companies: your employees' wages, benefits, safety and conduct.

5. **No authority to bind.** You cannot make promises or commitments on behalf of [Platform name], and it cannot do so on your behalf.

6. **Customer-facing summary** (shown on profiles and before contact):
   > "[Provider] is an independent service provider. [Provider] is responsible for the service it provides. [Platform name] provides the platform used to find and communicate with providers."
