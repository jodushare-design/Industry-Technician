## Draft plain-language text

**What [Platform name] is**

[Platform name] is a technology platform. It helps factories and industrial businesses in and around Hyderabad find and talk to independent technicians and industrial service companies.

**What the platform provides**

- Search for technicians and service companies by machine, skill and location.
- A shared catalogue of industrial machines and parts, for reference.
- In-app chat, with photos, videos and voice notes.
- A way for providers to send estimates, and for customers to accept, reject or ask for changes. Every version is recorded.
- A shared list for coordinating spare parts.
- A channel to raise complaints, which we acknowledge and follow up.

**What the platform does not do**

- We do not employ or send our own technicians.
- We do not diagnose machine faults or perform, supervise or inspect repairs.
- We do not guarantee the quality or result of any repair.
- We do not make or sell machinery, and we do not buy or sell spare parts.
- We do not collect payment for repairs or take a commission on them [currently].

**How it works between you and a provider**

- The customer chooses the provider.
- The provider decides how to assess and repair the machine.
- Customer and provider agree the scope and price directly.
- Any extra work needs the customer's new approval.
- Site access and safety arrangements are agreed between them.
- Payment is made directly to the provider.

**Verification**

Badges on a profile show only the specific check we carried out (for example, "Mobile verified" or "Certificate reviewed: [name], valid until [date]") and the date. A badge is not a guarantee of work quality.

**Our responsibilities**

We are responsible for:
- running the platform with reasonable care;
- protecting your data;
- accurately describing the checks we perform;
- handling complaints raised through the platform.
