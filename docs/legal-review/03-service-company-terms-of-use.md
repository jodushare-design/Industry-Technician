## Template outline

1. **Who we are and what the platform does.** As in Marketplace Service Description.

2. **Your status.** The company is an independent business.

3. **Employer responsibility.**
   - Technicians you add are your employees or contractors, **not** the platform's.
   - You are solely responsible for their:
     - hiring, supervision and pay;
     - statutory benefits;
     - training and competence;
     - safety;
     - conduct.

4. **Company administrator.**
   - Has authority to bind the company.
   - Manages member access.
   - Must remove members who leave.

5. **Company information.**
   - Accurate business details.
   - [GSTIN, registration number, address.]
   - Business documents may be reviewed. Badges show only what was reviewed.

6. **Employee profiles and skills.**
   - Company-level skills describe the company only.
   - You must record each employee's skills separately and accurately.
   - Don't assign an employee to work they aren't competent or licensed to do.

7. **Requests and assignment.** You decide whether to accept a request and whom to assign. The platform does not assign your staff.

8. **Licences and insurance.**
   - Hold the licences and registrations required for the work you offer. For example:
     - electrical contractor licence;
     - boiler repairer approval;
     - [others by category].
   - Hold the insurance required by law, e.g. employees' compensation. [Public liability recommended/required?]

9. **Work, estimates, parts.** Same obligations as in Technician Terms §6–8, applied to the company and its staff.

10. **Payment and taxes.** Direct with customer. Company is responsible for invoicing, GST and other taxes.

11. **Data.**
    - You control your employees' data that you enter.
    - You must have their consent or another lawful basis.
    - Use customer data only for the job.

12. **Complaints, suspension, liability, indemnity, changes, governing law.** As in Technician Terms.

## Counsel notes

- Who is liable for an employee's acts on a job: the company, as employer? Make the platform's position clear.
- Data-protection roles for employee data entered by a company admin.
