## Template outline

1. **What parts coordination is.**
   - A shared record for identifying, pricing, approving and tracking spare parts for a job.
   - The platform does not buy, sell, stock, deliver or recommend parts or suppliers. It takes no supplier commission [in V1].

2. **Who arranges parts.** Each part is marked "arranged by customer" or "arranged by provider". The party arranging it deals directly with the supplier.

3. **Compatibility.**
   - Must be confirmed by the responsible parties: the provider, and the supplier/manufacturer where relevant.
   - Each part shows one of these statuses: confirmed by provider, confirmed by supplier, unknown, not compatible.
   - The platform does not verify compatibility.
   - Catalogue information is reference only and does not establish compatibility.

4. **Genuine/OEM status.** Must not be claimed without a basis. The responsible party should keep evidence, such as supplier invoices.

5. **Customer approval of part costs.**
   - Each part cost needs the customer's in-app approval before it is charged.
   - Approved part costs are recorded separately from the accepted estimate.

6. **Supplier details.**
   - Recorded for coordination only.
   - Suppliers are not users bound by these terms unless registered.
   - [Supplier personal data — DPDP notice?]

7. **Warranty and returns.** Between the buyer and the supplier/manufacturer. The provider and customer should agree who handles returns of incorrect parts.

8. **Records.** Status history from "identified" to "installed" is kept and included in the completion report.
