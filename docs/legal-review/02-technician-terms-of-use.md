## Template outline

1. **Who we are and what the platform does.** Same as Marketplace Service Description.

2. **Your status.**
   - You are an independent service provider, not an employee, agent, partner or franchisee of the platform.
   - See the Independent Provider Relationship Statement.
   - You decide whether to accept requests, your prices, your methods, your hours and your tools.

3. **Eligibility.**
   - 18+.
   - Legally entitled to work in India.
   - Holds the qualifications, licences, registrations and insurance required by law for the work you offer (for example: [electrical contractor/supervisor licence for electrical installation work; boiler repairer approval for boiler work]).

4. **Your profile.**
   - Must be accurate.
   - Experience is shown as "stated by provider".
   - Do not describe yourself as working for or certified by the platform.
   - Do not claim certifications you do not hold.

5. **Verification.**
   - We may review documents you upload.
   - A badge shows only the specific check done and its date.
   - You must tell us when a licence or certificate expires or is withdrawn.

6. **Your work.** You are solely responsible for:
   - technical assessment and diagnosis;
   - work method;
   - competence;
   - suitable tools and PPE;
   - safe work practices;
   - following site rules agreed with the customer;
   - compliance with laws that apply to your work.

7. **Estimates.**
   - Must state scope, exclusions, charges and validity.
   - You may not start or charge for work beyond the approved scope without the customer's new in-app approval.
   - See the Service Estimate and Approval Terms.

8. **Spare parts.**
   - Identify parts accurately.
   - State compatibility honestly, including "unknown".
   - Don't charge for unapproved parts.
   - See the Spare-Parts Coordination Terms.

9. **Payment and taxes.**
   - The customer pays you directly.
   - You are responsible for invoicing, GST registration (if applicable), income tax and other statutory obligations.
   - The platform does not collect payments or take a commission [in V1].

10. **Platform fees.** [V1: none? Or subscription/listing fee — counsel and CA to advise on GST.]

11. **Conduct.** Acceptable Use and Prohibited Activities Policies apply. No:
    - harassment;
    - moving customers off-platform to avoid the approval record;
    - fake reviews;
    - sharing customer data outside the job.

12. **Customer data.** Use customer information only to provide the requested service. [DPDP role: is the provider an independent data fiduciary for customer data it receives? Counsel to decide.]

13. **Insurance.** [Recommend/require public liability and, where you engage helpers, employees' compensation cover. Counsel to decide whether mandatory.]

14. **Complaints against you.** You must respond through the platform within [x] days. We may share relevant complaint details with you and the customer.

15. **Suspension and termination.** For misconduct, unsafe practices, false documents, policy violations or legal requirements. [Notice and appeal process.]

16. **Limits of liability; indemnity.** [Counsel to draft. Indemnity for claims arising from your work, your breach or your violation of law.]

17. **Changes, governing law, disputes.** As in Customer Terms.

## Counsel notes

- Could providers be "platform workers" under the Code on Social Security, 2020? If so, what does that mean for these terms, registration and any aggregator contribution?
- Avoid control indicators that could suggest employment. Examples: mandatory hours, platform-set prices, platform performance targets, uniforms or branding.
