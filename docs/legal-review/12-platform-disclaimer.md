**Drafting rule:** this disclaimer explains the platform's role. It must **not** claim the platform has no liability, or that it removes liability the law does not allow to be excluded.

## Draft long form

[Platform name] is a technology platform that helps customers find and communicate with independent industrial service providers.

Providers are independent businesses. They are not employed, dispatched or supervised by [Platform name]. Each provider is responsible for:
- assessing the equipment;
- deciding how to carry out the work;
- the quality and safety of the work;
- holding any licences, qualifications and insurance the law requires.

[Platform name] does not diagnose machine faults, perform or supervise repairs, sell machinery or spare parts, or guarantee the result of any service.

**Agreements and site safety**
- Customers and providers agree the scope, price and payment directly.
- Customers are responsible for accurate machine information and for safe site access and site safety arrangements, agreed with the provider.

**Catalogue and badges**
- Catalogue information comes from the sources shown, is provided for reference, and may be incomplete. It does not confirm part compatibility.
- Verification badges describe only the specific check performed and its date.

**What [Platform name] is responsible for**
- running the platform with reasonable care;
- protecting your data;
- accurately describing its checks;
- handling complaints raised through the platform;
- any other obligations it has under law.

## Draft short form (screens)

> "Providers on [Platform name] are independent businesses responsible for their own work. [Platform name] does not perform or guarantee repairs."
