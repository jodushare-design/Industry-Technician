## Template outline

1. **Grievance Officer.** [Name, designation, email, phone, postal address.] Shown on the website and in the app.

2. **What you can complain about.**
   - (a) A provider's or customer's conduct on a job.
   - (b) Disagreement about an estimate, approval or part.
   - (c) Concerns about work quality.
   - (d) Safety incidents.
   - (e) Privacy or data issues.
   - (f) Platform/app problems.
   - (g) Content that breaks our policies.

3. **How to complain.**
   - In-app complaint form (linked to a job where relevant), or email to the Grievance Officer.
   - Include: job ID, category, description, attachments.

4. **Timelines (draft).**
   - Acknowledgement within **24 hours**.
   - Resolution or a reasoned response targeted within **15 days**.
   - [Counsel to confirm which statutory timelines apply. E-Commerce Rules and IT Intermediary Rules set different timelines; the product uses the stricter until confirmed.]

5. **What we do.**
   - Acknowledge.
   - Assign a support person.
   - Collect information from both parties.
   - Review the platform records (chat, estimates, approvals, parts decisions, status history).
   - Facilitate communication.
   - Record the outcome.
   - Take policy enforcement action where appropriate.

6. **What we do not do.**
   - We do not decide the technical cause of a fault.
   - We do not decide whether a repair was technically correct.
   - We do not award compensation between customer and provider [unless counsel advises otherwise].
   - We can share the relevant platform records with both parties for their own resolution or legal process.

7. **Safety incidents** (injury, fire, electric shock, explosion, serious equipment damage):
   - Contact emergency services (112) first.
   - Then report in the app.
   - The incident is escalated to a senior team member immediately.
   - Related records are preserved.
   - We co-operate with lawful requests from authorities.

8. **Record keeping.**
   - Complaints and evidence are kept for [x years].
   - They are not deleted because an account is suspended or closed.

9. **Escalation.** If you are not satisfied, you may [escalate to — senior contact] and you keep any rights you have under law (e.g. consumer commissions, courts).
