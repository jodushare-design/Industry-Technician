# Incident Escalation Procedure

> **DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.** An operational procedure for the platform's support team. Not legal advice. Statutory reporting duties must be confirmed by counsel (compliance-checklist §7).

**Applies to:** complaints in the **Safety incident** category, or any report of:
- injury or death;
- fire, explosion or electric shock;
- a gas or chemical leak;
- a structural collapse;
- serious damage to equipment or property;

connected to a job arranged through the platform.

**What the platform does and does not do:** the platform preserves records, keeps both parties informed, co-operates with authorities and takes action on platform accounts. It does **not** investigate the technical cause, assign blame, or tell anyone how to make a site safe. That belongs to the site occupier, the provider, insurers and the authorities.

## What happens automatically

When a complaint is opened as a safety incident:
1. Severity is **critical** and the complaint is **escalated**.
2. Every platform admin and support agent receives an **incident notification**.
3. The reporter is shown: *"If anyone needs help now, call 112."*
4. Job records are already tamper-evident and are never deleted, even if an account is suspended or closed. This covers messages, estimates (fingerprinted), approvals, parts, the completion report (fingerprinted), status history, complaint history and the audit log.

## Steps for the on-call staff member

| # | Within | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min of the alert | Open the complaint. Read the description and attachments. |
| 2 | 30 min | **Call the reporter** (phone number on the complaint page). Confirm people are safe and emergency services (112) were called where needed. Do not give technical or medical instructions; direct them to emergency services and their site safety officer. |
| 3 | 1 hour | **Acknowledge** the complaint in the admin portal (this records the time). Add an internal note of the call. |
| 4 | 1 hour | Contact the **other party** (provider or customer) and tell them an incident was reported. Ask them to keep their own records. |
| 5 | 4 hours | A **platform admin** decides whether to **suspend** the provider's or customer's account as a precaution (Providers → Decision → Suspend, with a reason). Suspension never deletes records. |
| 6 | Same day | Remind the site occupier (customer) that **statutory reporting** of accidents may be their duty under factory or occupational safety law. The platform does not file it on their behalf. Remind both parties to notify their insurers. |
| 7 | When asked | **Co-operate with authorities.** Provide records only on a lawful request (police, inspectorate, court). Log each disclosure as an internal note and in the audit log. Counsel to confirm the request process. |
| 8 | Ongoing | Keep both parties updated through the complaint (messages to parties). Record facts and actions, not opinions on cause. |
| 9 | When settled | Resolve with the right category (e.g. *Referred to the authorities*, *Account action taken*) and a handling note. Close a disputed job only after the complaint is resolved. |
| 10 | Within 7 days | Internal review: what the platform could improve (e.g. regulated-work warnings, checklist wording). No blame on individuals in this review. |

## Evidence

- Staff read a job conversation **only through the complaint** ("Job conversations" on the complaint page). Each view is recorded on the complaint and in the audit log.
- Do not download files to personal devices. Use the 2-minute signed links in the admin portal.
- Do not delete or alter anything. The database prevents it, and any attempt is an incident in itself.
